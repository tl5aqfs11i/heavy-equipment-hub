# Rear End Loader Truck - Technical Overview and Buying Tips

> 后装 end 装载 卡车

For buyers importing a rear end loader truck from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

The rear end loader truck is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Export models from Chinese manufacturers typically feature proven chassis options including Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW, ISUZU and MAN, with factory-direct FOB pricing that runs 30-40% below local dealer prices in most destination markets.

When sourcing a rear end loader truck from China, buyers should focus on three key areas: chassis reliability, body construction quality, and after-sales parts availability. Chinese export builds pair proven engines (Cummins, Weichai, ISUZU-derived units) with inexpensive spare parts networks in every major African and Asian port city, making them the dominant choice for fleet operators.

The rear end loader truck is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.
