For buyers importing a maximum capacity of 20ft container from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

When sourcing a maximum capacity of 20ft container from China, buyers should focus on three key areas: chassis reliability, body construction quality, and after-sales parts availability. Chinese export builds pair proven engines (Cummins, Weichai, ISUZU-derived units) with inexpensive spare parts networks in every major African and Asian port city, making them the dominant choice for fleet operators.

Proper maintenance of a maximum capacity of 20ft container centers on the PTO engagement system, hydraulic oil quality, and body mounting integrity. Most Chinese export models are designed for simple field maintenance with standard tools. A complete spare parts kit (filters, seals, hydraulic hoses) should be ordered with the initial purchase to ensure availability in remote operating locations.
