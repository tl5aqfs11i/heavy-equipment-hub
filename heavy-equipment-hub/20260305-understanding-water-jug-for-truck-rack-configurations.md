## FAQ

**Q: what chemical do mosquito trucks spray**

Mosquito trucks typically spray an insecticide formulated to control adult mosquitoes. The exact chemical depends on local regulations, target species, and application method, but common active ingredients include pyrethroids such as permethrin, resmethrin, or sumithrin. Some programs use organic or synthetic formulations designed for ultra-low-volume spraying. In certain cases, larvicides like Bti are used separately to treat standing water rather than adult mosquitoes. If you are sourcing a mosquito truck or spraying system for B2B use, the chemical choice should be matched to your local public health requirements and environmental compliance standards.

**Q: what's tipper truck**

A tipper truck, also known as a dump truck, is a special-purpose vehicle designed to transport loose materials such as sand, gravel, soil, coal, or construction waste. It is equipped with a hydraulic lifting system that raises the cargo bed so the load can be discharged quickly and efficiently from the rear or side. Tipper trucks are widely used in construction, mining, road maintenance, and agriculture. Depending on payload capacity, axle configuration, and brand, a new tipper truck typically costs around USD 40,000 to USD 180,000+, while used units may be lower. **Q: what does a tanker truck hold**

A tanker truck is designed to carry and transport liquids or gases in bulk. Depending on its configuration and industry use, it may hold fuel such as gasoline or diesel, water, chemicals, milk, liquid food products, or industrial gases. Some tanker trucks are also built for hazardous materials and must meet strict safety and regulatory standards. The exact capacity varies by truck size and tank design, but many tanker trucks can carry anywhere from a few thousand to over 10,000 gallons. If you need a specific type of tanker, the load capacity and material compatibility should be matched to the product being transported.

**Q: what is a car carrier truck called**

A car carrier truck is commonly called a **car carrier**, **auto transporter**, or **vehicle transport truck**. In the trucking and logistics industry, it is often referred to as a **car hauler**. These trucks are designed to transport multiple vehicles at once, usually on a specialized trailer with one or two levels. For single-vehicle transport, some people may also use the term **rollback tow truck** or **flatbed tow truck**, depending on the design. If you are sourcing this type of truck for commercial use, the exact name can vary by configuration and capacity, but “car carrier truck” and “auto transporter” are the most widely used terms.
