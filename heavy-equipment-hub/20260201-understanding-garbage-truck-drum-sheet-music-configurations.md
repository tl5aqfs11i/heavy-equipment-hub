## Field Notes

Garbage collection and population are closely related. As the garbage problem becomes significant with the development of the city, it is one of the problems that must be solved for urban development. In earlier years, as the world's population grew, sewers were inadequate, while garbage was dumped indiscriminately, leading to the spread of pathogens such as dysentery, cholera, and the Black Death. This experience gave rise to the world's first garbage collection service in the 18th century, in which garbage and excrement were collected on foot in collection carts. Dysentery was successfully suppressed with this method, but it did not work for other pathogenic bacteria. As a result, a covered trolley was developed in England in the 1920s. Soon, garbage collection became a labor-intensive industry.

A garbage truck is a truck specially designed to collect municipal solid waste and transport it to a solid waste treatment facility, such as a landfill, recycling center, or transfer station.
