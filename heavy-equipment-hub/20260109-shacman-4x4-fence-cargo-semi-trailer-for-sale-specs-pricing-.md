**Q: what is a side tipper truck**

**Q: what is compressed natural gas**

Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) is natural gas stored at high pressure, typically around 3,000–3,600 psi, so it can be used as a cleaner-burning vehicle fuel. In special trucks, CNG is commonly used in fleets that want lower emissions, reduced fuel costs, and quieter operation compared with diesel. It is mainly methane and is stored in strong cylinders on the vehicle. CNG trucks are popular for municipal, delivery, waste management, and regional transport applications. If you are considering CNG trucks, the total vehicle cost is usually higher than diesel models, but operating costs can be lower over time depending on fuel availability and duty cycle.
