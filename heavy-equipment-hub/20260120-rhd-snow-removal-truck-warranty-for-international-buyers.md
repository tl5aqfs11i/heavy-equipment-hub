## Field Notes

In practical terms, a typical snow removal truck is built on a 4x2, 6x4, or 8x4 heavy-duty chassis and fitted with a front plow, snow pusher, salt spreader, de-icing sprayer, or combination winter maintenance system. Common engine outputs range from 180 hp to 420 hp depending on chassis class and road duty. For medium municipal use, many buyers choose a 6x4 truck with 200 to 300 hp, a 5 to 8 ton payload for spreading equipment, and corrosion-resistant tank and hopper materials.

The market has shifted from simple plow-only units toward multifunction winter road maintenance trucks. This trend is driven by lower staffing levels, faster response expectations, and the need to handle both snow and anti-icing work with one vehicle. Many municipalities and contractors now ask for a single truck that can plow in the morning, spread salt in the afternoon, and support brine spraying before a freeze event.
