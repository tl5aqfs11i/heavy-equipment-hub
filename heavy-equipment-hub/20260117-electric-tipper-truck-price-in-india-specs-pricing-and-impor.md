**Q: what is an aerial lift platform**

An aerial lift platform is a mobile elevated work platform designed to safely raise people and tools to height for tasks such as maintenance, construction, inspection, and installation. It is commonly mounted on a truck chassis or trailer, and typically includes a boom, scissor mechanism, or articulated arm with a work platform or basket. These vehicles are used where ladders or scaffolding are not practical, offering better reach, stability, and efficiency. Depending on working height, configuration, and brand, prices for aerial lift platforms can range roughly from USD 20,000 to over USD 150,000.
