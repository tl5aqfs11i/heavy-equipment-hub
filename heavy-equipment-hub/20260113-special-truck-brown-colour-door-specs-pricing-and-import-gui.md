**Q: how to repair truck curtain**

Repairing a truck curtain depends on the type of damage. For small tears, clean the area, dry it fully, and use a heavy-duty PVC repair patch or welding kit designed for truck tarps. For loose seams, re-stitch with UV-resistant, high-tensile thread and reinforce the edge. If the curtain track, rollers, or tensioning system is damaged, inspect and replace the worn hardware first. For larger tears, cuts near load-bearing points, or repeated damage, professional repair or full panel replacement is recommended. Typical repair costs range from USD 50–300 for minor work and USD 300–1,000+ for major curtain or hardware replacement, depending on size and material.
