## Field Notes

To plan procurement properly, overseas buyers should match the truck to local snowfall conditions and road size. A light-duty snow removal truck with a Dongfeng chassis and basic plow is usually the most economical option for small cities or contractors. A mid-range truck with a stronger engine, corrosion protection, and salt spreading system is better for regular winter service. For airports, mountain highways, or large municipal fleets, higher-end imported chassis such as HINO or VOLVO may be preferred for durability and resale value.

Realistic specs for a standard export model are a 180–280 hp engine, manual or AMT transmission, 8–15 m³ salt spreader capacity, and a 2.5–3.5 m front plow width. A simple municipal model may start around **USD 35,000–55,000 FOB China**, while a more complete multi-function unit can reach **USD 60,000–110,000+** depending on chassis and auxiliary equipment.
