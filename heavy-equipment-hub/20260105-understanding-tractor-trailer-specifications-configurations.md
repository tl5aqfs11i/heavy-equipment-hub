## FAQ

**Q: how are containers attached to trucks**

Mechanically, a special truck is a chassis plus engineered body: the engine drives both the wheels and, through the PTO, the working system. Control is electro-hydraulic, with safety interlocks that stop the body when the truck moves. Chinese export models are designed for simple maintenance — most service points are reachable with standard tools, and factories ship a spare kit (filters, seals, hoses) with each unit.

**Q: how much does it cost for a full vehicle wrap**

A special truck from Chinese manufacturers is typically quoted between $28,000 and $85,000 FOB, depending on chassis brand, capacity tier and specification. Japanese-chassis builds (ISUZU, Hino) run about 25% higher, while European chassis (MAN, Volvo, Scania) add 55-65%. Ocean freight to most destinations adds $3,000-8,000 by RO-RO or container. Always ask for a formal quotation that lists destination port, emission standard and warranty terms before you commit.

**Q: how to operate snow plow truck**

Operating a snow plow truck requires proper training and careful control. Start by inspecting the truck, hydraulic system, plow blade, lights, tires, and fluids before use. Adjust the plow according to road conditions and ensure all safety equipment is working. Drive at a low, steady speed, keeping a safe distance from other vehicles and obstacles. Use smooth steering, braking, and lifting movements to avoid damaging the plow or truck. When clearing snow, make repeated passes if needed and watch for hidden curbs, manholes, or ice. Always follow local regulations and manufacturer instructions. For heavy-duty snow plow trucks, professional training is strongly recommended.
