## FAQ

**Q: do fire trucks need keys**

Generally yes, with one condition: buy from a real manufacturer, not the lowest quotation. A properly specced fire truck handles commercial duty cycles well; the failures you hear about usually trace to overloaded cheap builds with no parts support. Ask for the factory license, shipment history and a written 12-month warranty, and use third-party inspection before the balance payment.

**Q: do semi trailers have tractor protection valves**

Yes — provided you match the specification to the job and the supplier to reality. A semi trailer built on a Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a factory warranty is a proven workhorse across Africa, the Middle East and Southeast Asia. The checklist that matters: correct capacity tier, emission standard accepted by your country, pre-shipment inspection, and a spare-parts package in the container. Priced at $9,500-42,000 FOB, most buyers recover the investment within 18-30 months of operation.
