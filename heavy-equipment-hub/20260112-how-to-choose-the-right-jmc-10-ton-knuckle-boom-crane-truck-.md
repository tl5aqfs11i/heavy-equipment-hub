## FAQ

**Q: What are The Different Types of Work Trucks?**

Work trucks are specialized vehicles designed for specific job-site or service applications. Common types include pickup trucks for light-duty hauling and service work, flatbed trucks for transporting materials, dump trucks for construction and bulk unloading, box trucks for moving goods and deliveries, utility trucks for telecom, electrical, and maintenance crews, and crane trucks for lifting heavy equipment. Other common special trucks include tow trucks, refrigerated trucks for temperature-sensitive cargo, tanker trucks for liquids, and garbage trucks for waste collection. The right type depends on payload, cargo type, terrain, and the tools or equipment needed for the job.

**Q: how to box truck**

To box a truck, you typically need to convert a chassis cab or flatbed into an enclosed cargo body. The process includes selecting the right box size and material, building or installing the cargo box, adding a rear roll-up or swing door, reinforcing the frame, and making sure the suspension, brakes, and payload capacity match the new load. Many buyers choose a custom upfit from a truck body manufacturer or dealer. If you need a complete box truck conversion, pricing usually ranges from about USD 15,000 to 45,000 depending on truck size, materials, and features. Always confirm local registration and compliance requirements before installation.
