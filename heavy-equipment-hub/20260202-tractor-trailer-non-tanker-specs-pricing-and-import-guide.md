# Tractor Trailer Non Tanker: Specs, Pricing and Import Guide

> 半挂列车 non 罐车

Pricing for a tractor trailer non tanker varies significantly based on chassis origin, body capacity, and specification level. Chinese chassis builds offer the best value, while Japanese (ISUZU, Hino) and European (MAN, Volvo, Scania) options command premiums of 25% to 65%. Ocean freight by RO-RO or container adds $3,000 to $8,000 depending on destination port.

The tractor trailer non tanker is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.

The tractor trailer non tanker is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Export models from Chinese manufacturers typically feature proven chassis options including Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW, ISUZU and MAN, with factory-direct FOB pricing that runs 30-40% below local dealer prices in most destination markets.

When sourcing a tractor trailer non tanker from China, buyers should focus on three key areas: chassis reliability, body construction quality, and after-sales parts availability. Chinese export builds pair proven engines (Cummins, Weichai, ISUZU-derived units) with inexpensive spare parts networks in every major African and Asian port city, making them the dominant choice for fleet operators.
