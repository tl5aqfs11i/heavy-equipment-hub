# Man 4X2 R Price for International Buyers

> 曼 4x2 洗扫车 价格

Pricing for a MAN 4x2 r price varies significantly based on chassis origin, body capacity, and specification level. Chinese chassis builds offer the best value, while Japanese (ISUZU, Hino) and European (MAN, Volvo, Scania) options command premiums of 25% to 65%. Ocean freight by RO-RO or container adds $3,000 to $8,000 depending on destination port.

Proper maintenance of a MAN 4x2 r price centers on the PTO engagement system, hydraulic oil quality, and body mounting integrity. Most Chinese export models are designed for simple field maintenance with standard tools. A complete spare parts kit (filters, seals, hydraulic hoses) should be ordered with the initial purchase to ensure availability in remote operating locations.

For buyers importing a MAN 4x2 r price from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

## FAQ

**Q: what are medium heavy duty trucks**

Medium heavy-duty trucks are commercial vehicles designed to carry loads heavier than light-duty trucks but lighter than full heavy-duty class 8 trucks. They typically have a gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) of about 10,001 to 26,000 pounds. These trucks are commonly used for delivery, utility work, construction support, towing, and regional transport. Examples include box trucks, flatbeds, stake beds, and some vocational chassis. They offer a balance of payload capacity, maneuverability, and operating cost, making them suitable for businesses that need reliable transport without the size and expense of the largest trucks. Many models can be customized for specific industry applications.

**Q: does china have a car brand**

Yes — provided you match the specification to the job and the supplier to reality. A special truck built on a Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a factory warranty is a proven workhorse across Africa, the Middle East and Southeast Asia. The checklist that matters: correct capacity tier, emission standard accepted by your country, pre-shipment inspection, and a spare-parts package in the container. Priced at $28,000-85,000 FOB, most buyers recover the investment within 18-30 months of operation.
